Welcome

A central home for every sister church in the fellowship.

Covenant Connection Fellowship of Churches brings together sister congregations under one shared covenant — with one place to find fellowship-wide announcements, register as a new member or vendor, submit prayer requests, and reach fellowship leadership directly.

This organization-owned site replaces scattered communication through personal email accounts, giving every church, member, and visitor one consistent, professional point of contact with CCFC.
